# Screenshot notes

Captured on 2026-11-04 from the running Tab Flow Map extension in Chrome. The main screenshots use **Compact** mode and an English interface. The README also shows Detailed and Tree views, plus the available display locations.

## Research scenario

The starting question was how carry-on travelers use compression packing cubes. The actual session opened 16 tabs and followed real links across Google, Amazon, YouTube, Pack Hacker and a product manufacturer's website. Its longest visible chain has five levels:

1. Google search for compression packing cubes and carry-on reviews.
2. BAGSMART compression-cube review on Pack Hacker.
3. Aer Travel Kit 3 review, a related organizer.
4. Aer Pro Kit review, reached while exploring the organizer branch.
5. The Pro Kit product page on the manufacturer's website.

Other branches compare Amazon packing-cube listings, individual products, a brand storefront and a packing-cube comparison video. All graph relationships shown came from the real extension session. There are no seeded demo nodes or invented research findings. Research notes describe questions to investigate, not verified product conclusions.

## Additional mode captures

A second real session followed seven tabs: a Google search for `compression packing cubes carry on`, an Amazon TRIPPED listing, the Tripped carry-on product, Eagle Creek compression cubes, and three pages reached through the Tripped product and packing-guide links. The deepest chain is **Google → carry-on product → packing guides → carry-on packing article**. These are new captures, not alternative views of the original sixteen-tab session. The Detailed note records a question and planned checks, not a product conclusion.

The overlay capture was verified on the packing article. The carry-on product page in the same session displayed a blank overlay, so the screenshots do not imply that overlays work on every website; the README gives the side panel and separate window as fallbacks.
